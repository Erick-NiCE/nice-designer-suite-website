Design System Reference & Live Demo

Meet Lynn

Lynn is NiCE Designer Suite's own visual identity — the dark, gradient-driven, motion-rich language you're looking at right now. It isn't a system the Suite audits or converts other work against; SOL and Lyra still are. Lynn is the tool's own skin, documented here in the same token shape.

Tokens

Motion tokens

The category SOL and Lyra don't have. Every one of these is demoed live below.

lynn/motion/gradient-shift
13s diagonal drift across a multi-stop hero gradient.
lynn/motion/shimmer
4s linear gradient-text sweep on one or two key words.
lynn/motion/orb
Large blurred color blobs drifting behind hero content.
lynn/motion/card-tilt-spotlight
3D tilt (≤7°) plus a radial spotlight that follows the cursor.
lynn/motion/magnetic-button
Primary buttons drift toward the cursor, then spring back.
lynn/motion/plasma-lightning
Canvas plasma-ball filaments that bend toward the cursor, with drifting motes.
lynn/motion/liquid-fill
Matter.js particle pool that sloshes and fills a container on hover.
lynn/motion/scroll-reveal
translateY + opacity on enter, cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1).
