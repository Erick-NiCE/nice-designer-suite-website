# Hero Flows Research OS — v5
### Built for a Small Team · Minimum Viable Rigor · Maximum Decision Impact

---

## The Core Problem This System Solves

In a small team, the Research Lead is simultaneously:
- Running studies
- Maintaining the knowledge base
- Presenting to the organization
- Managing the research pipeline

A system that takes more time to maintain than to run research is not an OS.
It is a second job.

**v5 design principle:** Every tool must earn its place by saving more time
than it costs to use. If it doesn't — it's cut.

---

## Six Operating Principles

```
1. No research without a decision hypothesis.
2. No study without first checking existing evidence.
3. No finding without traceable evidence, defined scope, and a stated falsifier.
4. No decision without a named owner.
5. No closed study without a documented outcome — or an explicit reason why
   outcome cannot yet be measured.
6. No evidence claim without stating what would prove it wrong.
```

Principle 3 is active during analysis — not just a field to fill in.
Principle 6 is checked at synthesis, not at intake.

---

## Three Operating Modes

**Pick the mode before anything else.** Wrong mode = wrong amount of process.

| | **LITE** | **STANDARD** | **STRATEGIC** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **When** | Answerable from data or KB | Clear decision, medium stakes | Big Bet or direction-changing |
| **Effort** | < 2 days | 1–4 weeks | 1 quarter |
| **Minimum required** | Log → Evidence → Decision Log | Intake → Eval → Research → Evidence → Brief → Outcome | All tools |
| **KB update** | Optional | One entry minimum | Full update |
| **Time cost of the process itself** | < 30 min | < 3 hours total overhead | Proportional to study size |

**Rule:** Start with the lightest mode that answers the question with acceptable risk.
If you are spending more time on process than on research — downgrade the mode.

---

## What to Do When a PM Runs Research Without You

This happens. In a small team, it is not an exception — it is a pattern.

**Do not treat it as a failure. Treat it as a signal.**

→ If it happens once: offer to help frame the findings through the Evidence Chain.
   Add to Decision Log. Extract what's usable for the KB.

→ If it happens repeatedly: the Intake process has too much friction.
   Simplify it. The system is the problem, not the PM.

→ If the PM's findings are being used to make product decisions without
   confidence framing: this is the moment to add value — not by owning the
   process, but by adding the layer of "here's what this evidence can and
   cannot tell us."

Shadow research is not a threat to the Research Lead's value.
Unframed evidence used as fact is.

---

## The Mandatory 20% — Non-Negotiable in Every Mode

```
□ Check KB and existing data before starting
□ Name the decision and the decision maker
□ Verify the decision is not already made (Optionality ≥ 2)
□ Apply Principle 3 during analysis: trace every finding to evidence
□ Apply Principle 6 during synthesis: state what would prove each claim wrong
□ Document the outcome — including "no action" with a reason
```
