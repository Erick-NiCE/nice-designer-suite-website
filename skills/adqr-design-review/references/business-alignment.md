# Business Alignment

Generated by `npm run gen:adqr-refs` from the rubric `score_design_review` reads. Never hand-edit. Rubric by Prafull Mane (ADQR).

Does the design support the business goal? Each check needs its own context field; a missing field excludes that check, never a guess.

5 dimensions. Score each with exactly one band: 0, 2, 4, 6, 8 or 10.

## Business Goal `business_goal`

**Does the design directly support the stated business goal?**

Excluded by the tool unless it needs the Business Goal field.

_No written anchors for this check. Score conservatively and let the evidence carry the judgement._

Evaluate for:
- Is the business goal explicitly stated?
- Is the goal understandable?
- Does the primary functionality contribute to the goal?
- Is important functionality required to achieve the goal missing?
- Does the design contain functionality unrelated to the goal?
- Is there a clear connection between the business goal and the proposed experience?

Evidence to inspect: PM-provided business goal; Primary CTA/actions; Major UI capabilities; Workflow steps; Information presented.

Do **not** evaluate:
- Whether the business goal itself is strategically correct
- Whether the business will actually achieve the KPI
- Visual quality
- Technical feasibility
