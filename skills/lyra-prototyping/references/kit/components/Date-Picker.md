Component classes: `sol-button`, `sol-date-picker`, `sol-error-icon`, `sol-error-message`, `sol-error-message-container`, `sol-icon`, `sol-icon-button`, `sol-icon-host`, `sol-icon-pointer-events-none`, `sol-input`, `sol-input-container`, `sol-input-container-date-picker`, `sol-input-label-area`, `sol-input-wrapper`, `sol-label`, `sol-label-asterisk`, `sol-label-content`, `sol-label-text`, `sol-screenreader-only`, `sol-text-input`.

This is the markup Storybook renders, class names included. Copy it as is. Do not restyle it and do not rename its classes: the classes are how the audit recognizes a real component.
